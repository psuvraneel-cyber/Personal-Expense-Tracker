P.E.T. — Data Erasure Google Play Policy

This portal allows users of P.E.T. — Personal Expense Tracker to request permanent deletion of their account and all associated personal and financial data in accordance with Google Play's User Data policy.

1. In-App Deletion (Instant & Recommended)

If you have the P.E.T. app installed on your device, you can delete your data immediately without waiting for support processing:

  • Open P.E.T. on your Android device.
  • Navigate to Settings > Account Settings > Delete Account.
  • Confirm deletion using your biometric or device credentials.
  • All local SQLite encrypted databases, Firebase Cloud Firestore documents, and Authentication records are erased instantly.

2. Web-Based Deletion Request

If you no longer have access to the app, submit the form below or email psuvraneel@gmail.com with your registered Google / Firebase account email address. Requests are processed within 48 hours.

3. What Data Is Permanently Erased

  • Firebase Authentication Record: Email, User UID, and OAuth provider tokens.
  • Cloud Firestore Collections: All synchronized transactions, budgets, custom categories, saving goals, recurring rules, alerts, and tombstones.
  • Subscription Entitlements: RevenueCat subscriber record dissociation.
  • Local Application Storage: SQLCipher 256-bit encrypted database and SharedPreferences cache upon next app launch / local wipe.

⚠️ Irreversible Action

Once executed, account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Any active Google Play subscription should also be canceled separately in Google Play Store > Subscriptions.
